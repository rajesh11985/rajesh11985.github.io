What "private" actually means here

It's an easy word to put on a website, so here is the specific version. Your chats, recordings, study material and budget entries are stored on your device and encrypted with AES-256-GCM. They are not uploaded. We do not have a copy, which also means we cannot read them, sell them, or lose them in someone else's breach.

There is no analytics SDK in the app, no advertising identifier and no third-party tracker. There is no account to create.

The one time it uses the internet: the first time you open Chat or Study, OwnlyAI downloads its AI model — a file of about 586 MB — from Hugging Face. Download it on Wi-Fi. After that the AI runs on your phone, including in aeroplane mode. Your prompts are never sent anywhere.

Recording other people

OwnlyAI can record audio, and the law about that differs by country and by US state — some require everyone in the conversation to agree first. Please tell people when you're recording them. The app shows the same reminder before every recording.
