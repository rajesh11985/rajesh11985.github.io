Common questions

Why does it download such a large file?

That's the AI model itself — about 586 MB. OwnlyAI runs the model on your phone rather than sending your words to a server, which means the model has to physically be on the device. Download it once over Wi-Fi and it works offline from then on.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes, once the model has downloaded. Chat, Study, Record and Budget all work in aeroplane mode.

Where are my recordings?

On your phone, encrypted, listed under Library. They are written to disk every few seconds while you record, so an interruption or a crash costs you seconds rather than the whole session.

Please note that this version saves recordings but cannot yet play them back, export them, or turn them into text. If that's what you need, it's worth waiting for a later version.

Does OwnlyAI transcribe my meetings?

Not in this version. It records and stores audio securely; automatic transcription is not built yet. We'd rather say so plainly than imply a feature that isn't there.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for, and the app works fully without one.

Can I use it on Android?

Not yet — this release is iPhone only.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual items inside the app, or remove the app to delete everything. Since your content never reaches us, there is nothing on our side to delete.
