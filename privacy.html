Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

The short version

OwnlyAI keeps your content on your phone. Your chats, recordings, transcripts, notes and study sessions are stored on the device, encrypted, and are not uploaded to us. We do not have a copy. We cannot read them.

There is one exception, and it is yours to switch on: if you turn on Use online AI in Settings and add your own API key, what you type in Chat is sent to that service. Nothing else is, and it is off unless you choose it.

We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. No account is required.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only on your phone, encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM:

  • Chats you have with the on-device AI
  • Recordings you make, including the audio files themselves
  • Transcripts, summaries, action items, decisions and open questions
  • Notes you write
  • Study sessions, including topics and your self-assessments

Deleting the app removes this data from your device. You can also delete any individual chat, recording, note or study session from within the app. Because we never receive a copy, we cannot restore anything for you and we cannot delete it on your behalf — you are the only one who has it.

When the app uses the internet

OwnlyAI works offline. These are the only cases where it makes a network request:

Downloading the AI models

The first time you use Chat or Study, the app downloads a language model (about 586 MB) from Hugging Face. The first time you transcribe a recording, it downloads a speech model (about 148 MB) from Hugging Face. Both are plain file downloads. Your content is never sent, and both models run entirely on your phone afterwards — including in aeroplane mode.

Online AI, only if you turn it on

Settings has a switch called Use online AI when it is available. It is off by default. Turning it on shows a dialog explaining what happens, and requires you to add your own free API key from Google AI Studio or Groq.

  • What you type in Chat is sent to Google AI, or to Groq if Google is unavailable, using your key.
  • The request goes from your phone directly to that service. It does not pass through any OwnlyAI server.
  • Because the key is yours, your use of that service is governed by their terms and privacy policy, and their free-tier limits apply to your account.
  • Your recordings, transcripts, notes and study sessions are never sent, whatever this setting says.
  • Every answer in the app says which service produced it.
  • You can switch it off, or remove your key, at any time.

Reporting a crash or contacting support, only if you choose to

The app can open your own email app with a pre-filled message containing the error, the app version and your device model. Nothing is sent automatically — you see the message and decide whether to send it.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, no advertising identifier and no third-party tracker in the app.

If you email support, we receive what you write and your email address, and use it only to reply. We keep support emails for up to 12 months and then delete them. To have yours deleted sooner, ask at the address below.

API keys you add

If you add a Google AI or Groq key, it is stored in your device's keychain. It is never uploaded to us, never included in any report, and is sent only to the service it belongs to. Removing it in Settings removes it from the device.

Recording other people

OwnlyAI can record audio. Laws about recording conversations differ by country and, in the United States, by state — several require the consent of everyone being recorded. You are responsible for complying with the law where you are. The app asks you to confirm that you have informed participants before it will record a meeting or a lecture. Please tell people when you are recording them.

AI-generated content

Summaries, action items, transcripts and answers are produced by AI models and can be wrong, incomplete or misleading. Check anything important against the original recording or source. Do not rely on them for medical, legal, financial or other professional decisions.

Children

OwnlyAI is intended for adults and general use. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account — no sign-in is required.
  • Keep everything on this phone by leaving Use online AI off.
  • Delete any chat, recording, note or study session from within the app.
  • Delete everything by removing the app.
  • Ask us to delete support emails you have sent.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold. The only personal data we hold is what you send us by email.

Changes to this policy
