Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

The short version

OwnlyAI keeps your content on your phone. Your chats, recordings, study material and budget entries are stored on the device, encrypted, and are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not have a copy. We cannot read them.

We use no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. No account is required to use the app.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only on your phone, encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM:

  • Chats you have with the on-device AI
  • Recordings you make, including the audio files themselves
  • Study material you paste in, and the practice questions generated from it
  • Budget entries you add

Deleting the app removes this data from your device. Because we never receive a copy, we cannot restore it for you, and we cannot delete it on your behalf — you are the only one who has it.

When the app uses the internet

OwnlyAI works offline. There are only two cases where it makes a network request, and neither sends your content:

  • Downloading the AI model. The first time you use Chat or Study, the app downloads a language model file (about 586 MB) from Hugging Face, a third-party model host. This is a plain file download. Your prompts are never sent — the model runs on your phone afterwards, including in aeroplane mode.
  • Reporting a crash, only if you choose to. If the app crashes, it can open your own email app with a pre-filled report containing the error, your app version and your device model. Nothing is sent automatically — you see the message and decide whether to send it.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, no advertising identifier and no third-party tracker in the app.

If you contact support by email, we receive what you write in that message and your email address, and we use it only to reply to you.

Recording other people

OwnlyAI can record audio. Laws about recording conversations differ by country and, in the United States, by state — some require the consent of everyone being recorded. You are responsible for complying with the law where you are. Please tell people when you are recording them.

Children

OwnlyAI is intended for adults and general use. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account — no sign-in is required.
  • Delete any chat, recording or note from within the app.
  • Delete everything by removing the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
